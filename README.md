# Infinite Fuzion Hardcore Nuzlocke  
## About  
This will be a traditional nuzlocke with additional rulesets that impose restrictions on the 
Infinite Fusion aspect.  

## Rules
### Traditional Rules
1. Any Pokémon that faints must be released or boxed permanently. It is considered “dead” for the rest 
of the challenge.  
2. Only the first wild Pokémon encountered in a route or area can be caught. If the player fails to 
catch it (ie. it flees or faints), their opportunity to catch a Pokémon in that area is lost.  
3. The player must nickname all Pokémon they catch or obtain.  
4. You must choose your starting Pokeball without looking at the Pokemon.  
5. You must talk to your mom before departing.  

### Additional Rules
6. If you lose in the Lab, your run is over.  
7. If a pokemon becomes fused, it cannot become unfused.  
8. Only fused pokemon can be used in Gyms and the Elite Four.  
9. If you catch a fused pokemon, you can only keep 1 of the 2 aspects of the fusion.  
10. Once you enter a Gym, you aren’t allowed to leave it until you defeat the Gym Leader.  
11. Force yourself to battle every optional trainer possible before challenging the next Gym.  
12. As soon as you get a Pokeball, the Nuzlocke begins.  
13. Always combine movesets.  

### Exceptions  
1. Shiny Clause - If you encounter a Shiny Pokemon, you can catch it even if you have previously
encountered a Pokemon on that route.  
- You cannot actively search for a Shiny Pokemon.  
2. Duplicate Clause - Duplicates ARE allowed in this game mode since they can be fused with themselves
  or other Pokemon.   

## Game Settings  
1. All items are randomized, not 1 for 1.  
2. Pokemon cannot be levelled above the next gym.

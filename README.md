# Infinite Fuzion Hardcore Nuzlocke  
## About  
A modified set of Nuzlocke Rules to play a challenging Infinite Fusion Nuzlocke, while optimizing the game for enjoyment.

## Rules
### Core Rules
1. Any Pokémon that faints must be released or boxed permanently. It is considered “dead” for the rest 
of the challenge.
2. If you lose in the Lab, your run is over.  
3. Only the first wild Pokémon encountered in a route or area can be caught. If the player fails to 
catch it (ie. it flees or faints), their opportunity to catch a Pokémon in that area is lost.  
4. The player must nickname all Pokémon they catch or obtain.  
5. You must choose your starting Pokeball without looking at the Pokemon.  
6. You must talk to your mom before departing. (Optional)
7. As soon as you get a Pokéball, the Nuzlocke begins. 

### Additional Rules
7. If a Pokémon becomes fused, it cannot become unfused.  
8. If you catch a fused Pokémon, you can only keep 1 of the 2 aspects of the fusion. The unused aspect
   is considered "dead" and must be stored in the PC.
9. Only fused Pokémon can be used in Gyms and the Elite Four.
10. You must fight all trainers in a Gym before taking on the Gym leader.
11. Once you enter a Gym, you aren’t allowed to leave it until you defeat the Gym Leader.
12. You CAN purchase healing items from the Pokémart and use as necessary.

### Exceptions  
13. Duplicate Clause  
- Duplicates ARE allowed in this game mode since they can be fused with themselves
  or other Pokemon.  
- A player may choose to reroll their Pokémon on the route if they do not want to have a duplicate.


## Game Settings  
14. All items are randomized, 1 for 1.  
15. Pokemon cannot be levelled above the next gym.

# Game Modifications
16. The player can take the EXP ALL from Professor Oak's PC.  
17. Oak's Parcel is located in Professor Oak's PC to skip the first quest.
